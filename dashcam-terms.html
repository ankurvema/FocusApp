4. Driving safely

DashCam is a tool, not a safety system, and it does not make driving safe. Please:

  • Mount your phone securely, where it does not block your view of the road or your airbags, and where the law allows it.
  • Do not touch or watch your phone while driving. Start recording before you set off, or have a passenger do it.
  • Do not rely on driver monitoring. It looks only for eyes that stay closed, needs good light, and misses things. It is no substitute for stopping when you are tired.
  • Do not rely on impact detection. It can miss an incident or trigger when nothing happened, and DashCam does not call emergency services for you. If anyone is hurt, call them yourself.
  • Treat the speed, location and driving score as estimates. They come from your phone's sensors and are not a speedometer, a legal record or advice about speed limits.
